## 1. Method
- Reviewed all seven supplied papers: May–June 2023, November–December 2022, May–June 2024, November–December 2023, May–June 2025, November–December 2024 and November–December 2025.
- Counted each concept once per paper, even when repeated in the same paper.
- Included OR alternatives and sub-questions.
- Mapped questions to Unit V by their syllabus topic, not only by the question-number slot in which they appeared.
- Kept explicit “Selenium Tool Suite” questions separate from individual component questions.
- Mark values are copied from the supplied scans; one Selenium IDE question in November–December 2024 is 5 marks, while other listed questions are generally 6 marks.

## 4. Repeated-question analysis

### Strong repeated concepts
1. **Automated Testing Process:** Repeated in six papers with wording such as explain, construct or illustrate; the core steps remain the same.
2. **RPA:** Repeated in six papers as a direct explanation question.
3. **Performance Testing:** Repeated in six papers as definition, uses or explanation with example.
4. **Selenium IDE:** Repeated in five papers; OCR errors in some scans should be interpreted in context.
5. **Automation Benefits:** Repeated in four papers.
6. **Selenium WebDriver:** Repeated in four papers.
7. **Selenium Tool Suite:** Asked explicitly as an overall suite question in four papers.

### Lower-frequency concepts
- Tool selection and Selenium Grid: two papers each.
- Manual vs Automation and Selenium RC: one paper each.
- Apache JMeter: one paper, with explicit sub-parts for aim/purpose, working and advantages.

## 6. Preparation guidance
- Prepare the 6/7 and 5/7 topics thoroughly.
- Learn Selenium component distinctions, not just a generic Selenium definition.
- Prepare tool-selection factors and Grid architecture.
- Prepare short, accurate notes for RC and JMeter because they have appeared.
- Do not skip syllabus-only topics: Frameworks, SoapUI, Tosca, Appium and Cucumber.
- Treat the frequency table as historical evidence, not a guaranteed prediction.
