## Counting method

A topic is counted at most once per paper for its paper-level frequency, even if it appears in more than one sub-question in that paper. Exact wording and conceptually related questions are grouped transparently. Topics that appear in only one paper are retained where they are independently asked.

## Source set

Seven supplied SPPU STQA papers were reviewed, spanning May–June 2023 through November–December 2025. See the frequency-analysis file for paper codes and limitations.
