## 2. Frequency method

- Denominator: seven supplied papers.
- A concept is counted once per paper, regardless of how many sub-questions mention it.
- Conceptually equivalent wording is grouped only when it tests the same core topic.
- A combined question may contribute to more than one distinct concept (for example, Performance and Security Testing).
- Marks shown are the marks printed beside the relevant question in the source paper.
- The supplied papers mostly ask Unit III questions for 6 marks; this is an observation about this sample, not a universal rule.

## 4. Unit III question evidence (marks)

The Unit III questions listed in the reviewed papers are generally allotted 6 marks. The May–June 2024 paper includes the combined review-technique question for 6 marks; the November–December 2022 paper includes Path and Conditional Coverage together for 6 marks. Combined questions are not counted twice as separate occurrences of the same concept.

## 5. Cautions and limitations

1. This analysis covers only the seven supplied papers, not every SPPU paper ever issued.
2. Frequency is a retrospective count, not a prediction guarantee.
3. Some broad questions (for example, “Static Techniques”) cover multiple subtopics; they are not treated as four independent question occurrences.
4. Selenium was requested as an additional answer during preparation, but the supplied official syllabus places Selenium in Unit V. It is excluded from Unit III counts.
5. The Unit III exemplar on manual testing of an Online Marketing Software Platform and the decision-table fund-transfer case study remain syllabus topics even though they are not listed as repeated standalone questions in this summary.
