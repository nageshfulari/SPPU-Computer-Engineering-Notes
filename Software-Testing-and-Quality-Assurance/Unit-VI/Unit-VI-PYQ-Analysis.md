# Unit VI – PYQ Analysis
## Seven supplied SPPU End-Semester papers

**Subject:** Software Testing and Quality Assurance (410245-D)  
**Unit:** VI – Testing Framework

## 1. Method
- Reviewed the seven supplied papers listed in Section 3.
- Counted a topic once per paper, even if it appeared more than once in the same paper.
- Included OR alternatives and questions placed under different question numbers.
- Grouped related questions about Ishikawa tools and quality charts into one tool-family topic.
- Kept ISO 9000 / ISO 9001, maintaining SQA, achieving software quality and TQM as distinct topics.
- Marks are taken from the supplied scans and are reported as observed ranges where question wording or marks varied.
- OCR spelling such as “Detect Removal” is interpreted in context as Defect Removal Effectiveness (DRE).

## 4. Repeated-Question Analysis

### Very high frequency
1. **Ishikawa's Quality Tools / Quality Control Charts (7/7):** Appeared in every supplied paper. Questions include the overall tools, Flowchart vs Histogram, Run Chart vs Control Chart, and explanations of selected charts.
2. **Six Sigma (6/7):** Repeated as characteristics or strategy in SQA.
3. **ISO 9000 / ISO 9001 (5/7):** Appeared as a standard question in five papers.
4. **Maintaining SQA (5/7):** Repeated with similar direct wording.

### High frequency
5. **Achieving Software Quality (4/7):** Asked as activities, facts or parameters required to achieve high software quality.
6. **TQM (4/7):** Appeared as a direct explanation question.

### Moderate / lower frequency
- SQA Plan – 2/7 in the consolidated study notes; second occurrence needs verification against the paper scans.
- SQA Tasks, Goals and Metrics – 2/7.
- DRE – 2/7.

## 6. Preparation Guidance
- Prepare the quality-control tool family and its diagrams thoroughly.
- Practise the differences between Flowchart and Histogram, and Run Chart and Control Chart.
- Prepare Six Sigma, ISO 9000 / 9001 and maintaining SQA as high-priority topics.
- Prepare TQM and high-software-quality activities in 6–8 mark depth.
- Revise SQA tasks/goals/metrics and DRE with formulas.
- Cross-check the second SQA Plan occurrence against the original paper before relying on the 2/7 frequency.
- Treat frequencies as historical evidence from seven papers, not as a prediction or guarantee.
