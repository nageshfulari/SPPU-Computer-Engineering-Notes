## 2. Frequency method and cautions

- Denominator: seven supplied End-Semester papers.
- A concept is counted once per paper, even if it appears in multiple sub-questions.
- Related wordings are grouped only when they assess the same core concept.
- The May–June 2023 question on the impact of defects in different SDLC phases is grouped under the broader software-defect topic.
- The May–June 2024 Waterfall Model question is related to the software development process but is not identical to a general SDLC-steps question.
- Frequencies are counts from this sample, not predictions or guarantees.

## 4. Important interpretation

- **Strong recurring areas in this sample:** Software defects, QMS, ISO 9001 and CMM.
- **Repeated areas:** QA vs QC and customer satisfaction.
- **Appearing in two papers:** Quality Plan, SDLC problematic areas and quality control methods.
- **Lower-frequency questions remain part of the syllabus** and should not be ignored solely because they appeared less often.

## 5. Limits of this analysis

1. This analysis covers only the seven supplied End-Semester papers.
2. It does not represent every SPPU paper or guarantee future questions.
3. Some topics overlap. For example, a question on QMS pillars is grouped under the broader QMS concept.
4. Marks and wording may vary between examination sessions.
5. The Unit IV syllabus includes additional topics and two case studies that are not all represented in the recurrence table.
