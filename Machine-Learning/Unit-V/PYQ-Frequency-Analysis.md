## Preparation Guidance

1. Practise the K-Means numerical carefully and understand centroid updates.
2. Prepare K-Medoids and its difference from K-Means.
3. Draw and interpret a dendrogram for Hierarchical Clustering.
4. Learn DBSCAN terms: Epsilon, MinPts, Core Point, Border Point and Noise Point.
5. Understand how Spectral and Graph-Based Clustering use similarity relationships.
6. Prepare LOF and Isolation Factor / Isolation Forest for outlier detection.
7. Revise Elbow, Silhouette Score, Gap Statistic, Intrinsic and Extrinsic evaluation, and cluster optimization.
