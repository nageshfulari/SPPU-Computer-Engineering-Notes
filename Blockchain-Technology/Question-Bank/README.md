## Frequency interpretation

- **7/7:** Appeared in all seven supplied papers.
- **5–6/7:** Repeated frequently.
- **3–4/7:** Recurring topic.
- **1–2/7:** Limited appearances; still part of the syllabus and may be examinable.
- A frequency score is historical evidence, not a guarantee of a future question.
- Similar questions are not automatically exact repeats. For example, “Explain Proof of Work” and “Compare consensus algorithms” require different answer structures.

## Examination structure observed

All seven papers reviewed have:
- 70 marks and 2.5 hours.
- Eight numbered questions.
- Four compulsory choice groups: answer one from Q1/Q2, one from Q3/Q4, one from Q5/Q6, and one from Q7/Q8.
- Diagrams requested wherever necessary.

## Source and scope limitations

1. The question bank covers the seven supplied end-semester papers only; it does not include in-semester papers.
2. Frequencies are topic-level counts, not a prediction of marks or certainty.
3. The official syllabus extract currently available covers Units III–VI. Units I–II must be verified before producing a complete-subject prediction.
4. Question wording is transcribed from the supplied papers. Minor OCR/spelling noise may be normalized in headings, but the original intent and marks are retained.
