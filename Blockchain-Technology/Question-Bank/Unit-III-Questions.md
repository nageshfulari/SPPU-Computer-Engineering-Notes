3. Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET), even though it was not found in the seven supplied papers.

7. Corda, R3 and CorDapps.
8. IOTA and its role in IoT.
9. Permissioned vs Permissionless consensus.
10. Bitcoin mining, nonce, miners and block validation.

## Preparation note

Consensus algorithms are repeatedly asked in several forms: individual algorithm explanations, a list-and-explain question, and a comparison of multiple algorithms. Prepare both individual working mechanisms and a comparison table; one answer format cannot fully substitute for the other.
