## High-value preparation checklist

1. Ethereum: purpose, features, architecture/components and how it differs from Bitcoin/traditional cryptocurrencies.
2. Ethereum network types: Mainnet, Testnet and Private Network (retain the terminology used by the question).
3. Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM): role and smart-contract execution.
4. Smart contracts: definition, purpose, types, working and examples.
5. Smart-contract implementation and deployment using Solidity.
6. Gas and its significance in execution.
7. Swarm: decentralized storage platform.
8. Whisper: decentralized messaging platform.
9. Truffle: development environment and basic workflow.

## Important distinction

Swarm and Whisper are different syllabus topics:
- **Swarm** concerns decentralized storage.
- **Whisper** concerns decentralized messaging.

Do not combine their functions in one answer. Truffle is explicitly present as a syllabus exemplar even though it did not appear as a standalone question in the seven supplied papers.
