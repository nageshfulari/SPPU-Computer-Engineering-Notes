## Answer-writing approach for case studies

For each sector-specific question, structure the answer around:
1. Existing problem in that sector.
2. How blockchain is applied.
3. Participants and data/transaction flow.
4. Role of distributed records, transparency, traceability and access control.
5. Sector-specific benefits.
6. Limitations or implementation challenges.
7. A simple labelled architecture or workflow diagram when useful.

Avoid repeating generic blockchain advantages without explaining the actual sector workflow.
