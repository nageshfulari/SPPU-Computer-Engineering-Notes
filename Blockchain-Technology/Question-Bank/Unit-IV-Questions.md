## Technical accuracy reminders

- A wallet does not physically contain coins. It manages keys used to access and authorize transactions recorded on a blockchain.
- Distinguish a wallet application from a cryptocurrency exchange and from the blockchain network.
- Never include real private keys, seed phrases or credentials in notes or examples.
- Zerocoin/Zerocash appeared in one supplied paper but is not named in the provided Unit IV syllabus extract. Treat it as supplementary unless the complete official syllabus or teacher material confirms it.
