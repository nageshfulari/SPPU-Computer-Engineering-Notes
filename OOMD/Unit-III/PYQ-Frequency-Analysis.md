## 5. Preparation Recommendations

1. Learn the notation and construction method for Use Case, Sequence, Activity and State Diagrams.
2. Practice broad concepts first, then apply them to the exact scenarios in the question bank.
3. Prepare Composite States and Class/State Model relationship as concise theory answers.
4. Practice the 8-mark diagrams: ATM activities, Email Login, Two-Wheeler Sales and Telephone Line Connection.
5. Keep the Coffee Vending Machine exemplar in view; the beverage purchase use case is directly relevant to the Unit III exemplar.
