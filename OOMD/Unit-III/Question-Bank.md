## Consolidated Topic Groups

2. **Use case modeling:** Actor, use case, system boundary, relationships, ATM and online reservation diagrams, beverage purchase use case description.
3. **Sequence modeling:** Sequence model, interaction diagram, sequence components, ATM, online shopping and rent-a-car sequences.
4. **Activity modeling:** Activity definition, branches, concurrency, ATM activities, ATM activity diagram, email login, hospital management and two-wheeler sales.
