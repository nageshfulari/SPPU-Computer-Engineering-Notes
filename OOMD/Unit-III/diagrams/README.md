## Diagram conventions
- Mermaid sequence diagrams show participants and messages in chronological order.
- Mermaid flowcharts represent the workflow, decisions and alternative paths of activity diagrams.
- Mermaid state diagrams represent states and event-triggered transitions.
- Use-case diagrams use Mermaid flowcharts for GitHub-native rendering; use-case meanings and relationship directions are explained in the answers.
- The diagrams are kept compact to support exam revision and hand-drawing.

**Important:** Mermaid is used for convenient GitHub rendering. Where strict UML symbols are required, the exam drawing should follow the UML notation explained in the answer.
