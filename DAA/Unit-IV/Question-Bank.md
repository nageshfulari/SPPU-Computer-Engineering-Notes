- Practise the graph-coloring and sum-of-subsets questions with the actual input supplied in the paper.
- For Least-Cost search, write the control abstraction and explain the node-selection rule.
- For TSP and knapsack, show the bound calculation and node-pruning decisions, not only the final answer.
- The airline crew scheduling answer is a general case-study model; use any exact constraints provided by the question or prescribed study material.
- Historical appearance does not guarantee an exact repeat.
