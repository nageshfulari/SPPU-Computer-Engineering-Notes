## 4. Observations

- Branch-and-Bound control abstraction and Least-Cost search appear in both directly inspected papers from 2022 and 2023.
- Backtracking is tested both as a general control-abstraction question and through concrete problems such as graph coloring and sum of subsets.
- Numerical/state-space problem-solving is important: prepare complete traces, not only definitions.
- 8-Queen remains in the supplied syllabus even though the earlier seven-paper review did not identify it in those papers.
- Airline Crew Scheduling is explicitly in the syllabus as an exemplar. Its absence from the reviewed question excerpts should not be treated as proof that it cannot be asked.

## 5. Important caution

Frequencies are historical counts from the earlier seven-paper review, not probabilities. The exact question, marks and internal choice can change in future examinations. Prepare the entire syllabus and use frequency only to prioritize revision.
