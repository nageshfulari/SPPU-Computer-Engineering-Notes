## 5. Exact / concept / topic repeat distinction

- **Exact repeat:** Same or almost identical wording and task. No exact-repeat claim is made here without matching the wording across papers.
- **Concept repeat:** Different wording tests the same underlying idea, such as explaining the greedy control abstraction and analysing its time complexity.
- **Topic repeat:** Different problem instances or question forms test the same topic, such as different 0/1 Knapsack instances or different matrix-chain dimensions.

Examples:
- Matrix-chain multiplication numerical problems are topic repeats; their dimensions and optimal parenthesization differ.
- Job sequencing questions with different deadlines and profits are topic repeats.
- Principle of Optimality and optimal-substructure questions are concept-related, but not necessarily exact repeats.
