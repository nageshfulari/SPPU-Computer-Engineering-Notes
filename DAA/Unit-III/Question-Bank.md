## How to use this question bank

- Prepare algorithm-and-numerical questions with full working, not only definitions.
- For repeated topics, practise a fresh input instance so that the method is understood.
- Do not interpret a topic repeat as an exact repeat.
- The marks shown are historical question-paper marks, not a prediction or guaranteed future allocation.
