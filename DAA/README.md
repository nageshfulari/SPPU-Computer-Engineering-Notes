## Preparation conventions

- Answers follow the SPPU exam-oriented structure and are sized according to the marks.
- Mathematical expressions use plain keyboard text for easy editing in MS Word.
- Algorithms use readable pseudocode and examples include intermediate working.
- PYQ frequency is counted by examination paper; related questions are classified as concept or topic repeats, not exact repeats.
- The supplied syllabus is the boundary for coverage. Case studies or topics without a prepared answer are explicitly identified rather than silently omitted.
